    What a support is
    
  
    Supports change how information and interaction are presented. They never infer anything about you. Some of them deliberately hold detail back or narrow a list of options — those say so on the label, before you choose them.
    
  
    This page follows the rules it is collecting
    
  
    No meaning is carried by colour alone, nothing animates, nothing is required, every choice is reversible, and no question asks how anything feels. The provenance of each support — what it does and how good the evidence for it is — sits behind “Why is this offered?” rather than on the face of the choice. The preview is a real sample answer rebuilt from your selections; it demonstrates some of the supports. Every chosen support is included in the exported instructions.
